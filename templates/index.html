MAKE IT RELEVANT

Your keyword watchlist

Match words or phrases against titles, excerpts and organisations. Try “construction”, “ICT”, “vehicles” or your district.

Matches appear in the sidebar and can trigger desktop alerts. Email and SMS alerts are not available.

DON’T MISS A DEADLINE

Desktop alerts

After each fetch, Windows shows one notification summarising what is new. Click it to open your desk.

Your watchlist is empty, so keyword alerts will stay quiet. Add keywords or choose “Any new open opportunity”.

Alerts appear only while the app (or a scheduled run.py --fetch) is running. Check Windows Settings › Notifications and Focus/Do not disturb if nothing appears.

KEEP YOUR DESK UPDATED

Automatic fetching

Fetches run while the Python application and this computer are running. You can close the browser.

Your records stay with you

SQLite stores first-seen dates, publication dates, source links, deadline evidence, saved items and your notes. Refreshes preserve your annotations. Export your current filtered view from the opportunity feed.

For unattended operation after a restart, see README.md for the Windows Task Scheduler setup. This is a personal local application; internet hosting needs authentication and a production server.
